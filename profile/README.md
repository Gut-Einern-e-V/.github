## 🌱 Who We Are

[Gut Einern e.V.](https://www.gut-einern.org/) is a community-driven non-profit founded in **September 2021** in Wuppertal, Germany. We transform an old farm estate into a **regional transformation centre** for sustainability, circular economy, and open innovation — where people, nature, and technology thrive together.

Our work spans:

- 🥦 **Permaculture garden & vertical farm** – growing food locally and sustainably
- 🔧 **Maker lab & open workshop** – hands-on fabrication and prototyping for everyone
- 🏡 **Coworking & community spaces** – open kitchen, meeting rooms, and shared infrastructure
- 🎓 **Akademie auf Gut Einern** – workshops, courses, and learning formats for all ages
- ☀️ **Renewable energy** – solar panels and ecological building practices on-site

---

## 🔗 Part of Germany's First FAB Region

We are a founding member and core actor of the **[FAB.Region Bergisches Städtedreieck](https://www.fab-bergisch.org/)** — **Germany's first FAB Region**, part of the global [Fab City](https://fab.city/) movement.

Together with partners across the **Bergisches Städtedreieck** (Wuppertal · Remscheid · Solingen), we are building a **collaborative, circular, and locally productive region** that shares knowledge and tools openly:

| Partner | Focus |
|---|---|
| Gut Einern e.V. | Sustainability hub & education |
| Gründerschmiede Remscheid | Entrepreneurship & making |
| Gläserne Werkstatt Solingen | Digital fabrication |

The FAB Region framework is rooted in the principle of moving cities and regions from **"importing everything"** to **"producing locally"** — reducing environmental footprints while strengthening resilience and community ownership.

## 💚 Our Commitment to Open Source

We believe that **open knowledge is a commons**. Everything we build, design, and learn is meant to be shared freely so others can adapt, improve, and build on it. This is why we embrace open-source culture across all our projects:

- 📖 Open educational resources and workshop materials
- 🛠️ Open hardware designs from our maker lab
- 🌐 Open data and transparent reporting
- 🤝 Collaboration over competition — with local communities and globally

All our software projects on GitHub follow open-source licensing principles. Contributions, feedback, and forks are always welcome!
